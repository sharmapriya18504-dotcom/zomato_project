Strategic Business Applications for Zomato

Targeted Budget Campaigns

Restaurants in the Budget cluster (₹150-₹800) attract students & young professionals. Ideal for daily lunch deals and quick delivery promotions.

Mid-Range Dining & Family Buffets

The Mid-Range tier (₹850-₹1,400) represents popular corporate favorites, family dining, and weekend buffet destinations like Barbeque Nation.

Zomato Gold & Fine Dining

The Premium cluster (₹1,500+) drives high average order value (AOV). Prime candidates for Zomato Gold table bookings and curated nightlife guides.
